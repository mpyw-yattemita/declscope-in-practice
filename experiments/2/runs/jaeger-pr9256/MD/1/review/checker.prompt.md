0. pkg/filter.go:49: The methods use different receiver kinds. IsEmpty has a pointer receiver and is safe on nil, but SupportsLevel and SupportsOperator have value receivers. SearchCapabilities.Filter is a *FilterCapabilities that is nil by default, so caps.Filter.SupportsOperator(op) panics with a nil dereference whenever a reader leaves Filter unset.
